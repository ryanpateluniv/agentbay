# Trip Planner

**Summary:** Plans a multi-day trip through a short chat: you pick from three outlines and approve the final itinerary.

## Description

Tell the planner where you want to go, for how long, your budget and what you enjoy. It drafts three itinerary outlines, lets you choose one, builds a detailed day-by-day plan and saves it after you approve. It uses the model's general knowledge unless live web search is available, and says which one it used.

## Example tasks

1. Plan 4 days in Lisbon for two people, mid-range budget, we love food and history
2. Weekend trip somewhere warm, budget friendly
3. Plan a 5-day Kyoto trip focused on temples and gardens
