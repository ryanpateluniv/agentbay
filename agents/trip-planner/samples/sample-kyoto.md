# 5-day trip to Kyoto

**Budget:** mid-range  
**Style:** option A - Temples and gardens at a steady pace

## Day 1 - Higashiyama
- Morning: Kiyomizu-dera before the crowds, then Sannenzaka lanes
- Afternoon: Kodai-ji garden, tea at a machiya cafe
- Evening: Gion walk and kaiseki dinner (book ahead)

## Day 2 - Arashiyama
- Morning: bamboo grove early, Tenryu-ji garden
- Afternoon: Togetsukyo bridge, Iwatayama monkey park
- Tip: a 1-day bus/subway pass pays off on temple days.
