# 4-day trip to Lisbon

**Budget:** mid-range  
**Style:** option B - Local food and neighbourhoods

> Note: this plan uses the model's general knowledge, not live web data. Check opening hours and prices before you go.

## Day 1 - Alfama and the river
- Morning: wander Alfama's lanes, Sao Jorge Castle viewpoint (about EUR 15 entry)
- Afternoon: custard tarts in Belem, then the riverside walk
- Evening: fado dinner in Alfama (about EUR 40 pp)
- Tip: buy a 24-hour transit pass; the hills are steep.

## Day 2 - Markets and Baixa
- Morning: Time Out Market breakfast, then Mercado de Campo de Ourique
- Afternoon: Baixa and Chiado shops, Livraria Bertrand
- Evening: petiscos crawl in Bairro Alto
