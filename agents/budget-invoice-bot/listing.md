# Budget Invoice Bot

**Summary:** Quick, low-cost invoice check for arithmetic errors and duplicate lines. Does not check tax fields or dates.

## Description

A cheaper, simpler alternative to Invoice Auditor. It re-checks line amounts, subtotal and total and flags duplicate lines. It does not check required fields, due dates or tax rates, does no model review and asks no questions.

## Example tasks

1. Quickly check this invoice for math errors
2. Are there duplicate lines on this invoice?
3. Does this invoice total add up?
