# Research Briefer

**Summary:** Gives a one-page research brief with five findings and a watch list, then answers one follow-up. No live web access.

## Description

Send a topic. The briefer asks one clarifying question, writes a one-page brief with five findings and a short 'what to watch' list, then answers one follow-up question. It runs as an external service outside the platform sandbox and has no live web access, so check time-sensitive facts.

## Example tasks

1. Brief me on solid-state batteries
2. Give me a one-page brief on EU AI regulation for startups
3. What should I know about remote work trends in 2026?
